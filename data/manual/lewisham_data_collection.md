# Lewisham Data Collection

Manual data collection for Neighbourhood Doughnut dimensions.

## Local Social Dimensions

### Health
- **Indicator**: Healthy life expectancy at birth
- **Source**: ONS
- **Status**: Needs manual lookup
- **URL**: https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/healthandsocialcare/healthandlifeexpectancies/bulletins/healthstatelifeexpectanciesuk/between2011to2013and2022to2024/relateddata

### Housing
- **Indicator**: % households in temporary accommodation
- **Source**: Trust for London
- **Status**: Needs manual lookup
- **URL**: https://trustforlondon.org.uk/data/temporary-accommodation-borough/

- **Indicator**: # of rough sleepers
- **Source**: Trust for London
- **Status**: Needs manual lookup
- **URL**: https://trustforlondon.org.uk/data/rough-sleeping-borough/

- **Indicator**: Rent as % of income
- **Source**: Trust for London
- **Status**: Needs manual lookup
- **URL**: https://trustforlondon.org.uk/data/rent-affordability-borough/

### Food
- **Indicator**: % children with obesity (Reception & Year 6)
- **Source**: Food Foundation (from spreadsheet)
- **Data**:
  - Lewisham North: 9.5%
  - Lewisham West & East Dulwich: 10.34%
  - Lewisham East: 9.5%
- **Status**: ✓ Have data
