Investment in Council Housing Using Warm Homes Social Housing Fund Wave 3

January 29, 2025 Mayor and Cabinet (Other) Key decision Approved View on council website

This summary is generated by AI from the council’s published record and supporting documents. Check the full council record and source link before relying on it.

Summary

...to approve a £11,441,176 funding application for retrofitting council housing and delegate grant agreement approval to the Executive Directors of Housing and Corporate Services.

Full council record

Decision

It was MOVED, SECONDED and RESOLVED:
 

   
i.                 
To approve the application for £11,441,176 funding under the
Warmer Homes Social Housing Fund as part of a three-year programme
valued at £20,882,353 to retrofit 1,100 properties within the
Council’s housing stock, and

  
ii.                 
To delegate approval to the Executive Director of Housing, in
consultation with the Executive Director for Corporate Services, to
enter into the grant agreement.
