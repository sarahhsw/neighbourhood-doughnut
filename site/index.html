About This Project

This pilot project visualizes ward-level data across the four lenses of Doughnut Economics:

  • Local Social Foundation (15 dimensions) - Health, Housing, Education, etc.
  • Local Ecological Ceiling - Green space, air quality, biodiversity
  • Global Ecological Ceiling - Climate, materials, planetary boundaries
  • Global Social Foundation - International supply chain impacts

Phase 1 Status: Global lenses implemented with London City Portrait data. Local lenses in development using ONS, GLA, DfE, TfL, and other official sources.

Note: This is a pilot implementation. Data quality and coverage vary by dimension. See individual dimension cards for geography level and confidence ratings.

Built on: Kate Raworth's Doughnut Economics | C40 Thriving Cities Initiative | London City Portrait 2024

Data sources: ONS, GLA Datastore, DfE, OHID, TfL, DWP, and others (see dimension details)
